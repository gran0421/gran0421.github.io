Lifestyle of the Arctic Fox

The Arctic Fox is a small, hardy mammal that lives in the Arctic regions of the world. It has a thick, white coat that helps it blend in with the snow and ice. The Arctic Fox is a skilled hunter, feeding on lemmings, voles, and other small mammals. It is also known for its ability to survive in extreme cold temperatures.

Facts about the Arctic Fox

  • Scientific Name: Vulpes lagopus
  • Habitat: Arctic tundra and coastal areas
  • Diet: Small mammals, birds, fish, and carrion
  • Lifespan: 3-6 years in the wild
  • Adaptations: Thick fur, keen sense of smell, and ability to change coat color with the seasons
